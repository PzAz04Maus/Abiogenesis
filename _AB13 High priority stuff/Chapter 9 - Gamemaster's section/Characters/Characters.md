# Colleagueships and "mafias" - https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaboration

>The word "college" is from the [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin "Latin") verb _lego, legere, legi, lectum_, "to collect, gather together, pick", plus the preposition _cum_, "with",[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College#cite_note-4) thus meaning "selected together". Thus "colleagues" are literally "persons who have been selected to work together". In [ancient Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome "Ancient Rome") a _[collegium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collegium_(ancient_Rome) "Collegium (ancient Rome)")_ was a "body, guild, corporation united in colleagueship ... ; a political club or trade guild".[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College#cite_note-5)

A collegueship is a group of individuals, or colleagues marked with a strong bond through a shared education, belief, project, or cause. Unlike projects, a colleagueship is not limited to a single operation, but The main difference between an authority figure with his students and a colleagueship is that colleagues tend to have no central mentor, achieve peer or near peer status to the mentor in the circle, or are otherwise instrumental to the colleagueship.
