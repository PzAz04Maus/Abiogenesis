Maus — 1:10 PM
Depends on context. If it's just a trailcrawl, then all that matters is pace, which is either distance or time. 

If the GM wants a competitive element, they have the party roll a test where the result determines the amount of time wasted.

If the GM wants player agency, they have the party roll a tradecraft test which can grant them the ability to form an alternative path with its own peculiar behavior. 

To make this into gameplay, the GM awards the players with bonuses to picking terrain conducive to the type of behavior they seek, and deducts penalties when they pick terrain that isn't conducive. For example, if the party seeks a shortcut, they can test their luck.
