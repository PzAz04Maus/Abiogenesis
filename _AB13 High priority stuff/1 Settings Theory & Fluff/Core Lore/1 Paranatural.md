# The Cold Story
While the de Kuiper Event punctured the old normal of life on Earth, many of the fields of study dedicated to supernormal phenomena had roots before it.

One major example was Soviet Paranormal research. Beginning in the 1920s in topics such as telepathy and ESP, the Soviet Academy of Sciences eventually backed multiple projects with statistically relevant results. However, Lacking efficient means to recreate supernormal phenomena, these efforts in studying the paranormal were riven with unreliability and fringe theory. [^2]

 Another major example was the US Stargate program of 1977 and its precursors,  'Gondola Wish', 'Stargate', 'Grill Flame', 'Center Lane', 'Project CF', 'Sun Streak', and 'Scanate.' created to investigate the potential for applying psychic phenomena to military and domestic intelligence applications, the project was terminated in 1995 after a CIA report concluded it was never useful in any intelligence operation.[^1]
