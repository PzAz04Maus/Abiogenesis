
The advantage of Liters and kg is that we have a standardized basis to work with:
- a volume of 1 liter of water is 1 kg
- 1 liter of water is 1000 cm^3
- the human body has slightly less density than water
- Human form factor is as much about volume

# Designer notes

- Rifle volumes are measured by a bounding box of their largest dimensions.
	- ==Do we add or remove the magazine?== prolly not
- Standard rifle magazines are standardized to 1 L volume #review
- Box of ammunition is pi(R)^2 * L * 110. The additional 10 is for 10% packaging purposes
