The ratio of a mammal's weight to its volume is related to its density. Since the density of protoplasm (the material within cells) is roughly constant across mammals, their weight (mass) is approximately proportional to their volume. This relationship is important in understanding how metabolic rate scales with body size, as described by Kleiber's law.
