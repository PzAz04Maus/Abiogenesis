# [Sapience article](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Sapience)

- **Sapience** is the capacity for advanced [thinking](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Thought "Thought"), [intelligence](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence"), wisdom, comprehension, rationality, emotion, [consciousness](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness") and self-awareness.
- **Sentience** is the capacity (via a [nervous system](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Neural_network "Neural network")) of a living thing to experience sensations such as pleasure or pain. These beings have an awareness of existence, and not necessarily **self-awareness** which is the experience of one's own personality or individuality.
- **Sophonce** is "sophisticated sapience" with metacognition: self-awareness, self-reflection, and the ability to think about one's thinking and correct errors that will lead to an [ELE](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Extinction_Level_Event "Extinction Level Event") for example.
- **Transapience** is a mental state that usually starts prior to sophonce but has the potential to exceed it by far. These are [AI](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Intelligence "Artificial Intelligence") that have usually [evolved](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Superintelligence "Superintelligence") from beyond a [singularity](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Technological_Singularity "Technological Singularity")
- species that have undergone accelerated evolution or _provolution_ (pro-active evolution using technology to enhance or augment the [intelligence](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Intelligence "Intelligence") of a species) or _prevolution_ (using provolution on dolphins as an example to enhance them into talking, land-dwelling species)
- A pre-sapient species can be _[uplifted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uplift_(science_fiction))_ by cultural, technological, or evolutional interventions like genetic engineering until it is sapient.
- The ultimate transapient state is [ascendance](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Ascended "Ascended") or transcendence where beings evolve into pure [energy](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Energy "Energy") and can become dimensional, ever ascending beyond the trappings of their physical bodies and limitations,



# [Type 0 article](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Type_0)

- A **Type 0 or K0** civilization extracts its [energy](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Energy "Energy"), information and raw-materials initially from crude organic-based sources (i.e. food/wood/fossil fuel);
- Energy usage: $10^6$ .. $10^{16}$ W
	- metabolism, water and wind, combustion, electricity, fossil fuels, gas and oil, fission
- population: 10$^{10}$
- Structures: 1 [reality](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Realm)

# Reality

- A **realm** is a structure that contains planes of existence.
- The material or physical realm of [reality](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Reality "Reality") contains [all planes of known existence](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Everything "Everything") within the 11 [dimensions](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Dimension "Dimension") of our [omniverse](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Omniverse "Omniverse"). Outside of reality it is unbound by dimensions.

- A [verse](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Verse "Verse") is a cosmological structure that holds [everything](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Everything "Everything") in existence. This ranges from a [universe](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Universe "Universe") to the [outerverse](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Outerverse "Outerverse").
- The difference between the 1st Realm (Reality) and the 2nd Realm is so vast that the 2nd is only beginning to be understood by a [Type II](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Type_II "Type II") civilization and only accessible _beyond_ [Type VII](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Type_VII "Type VII").
- Surreality is an [eternal](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Eternity "Eternity") realm between reality and non-reality (the afterlife) where life forces (souls) may wander in limbo, or move on to the afterlife. This is the realm of dreams linked to the unconscious or subliminal mind. 
	- This is also the realm (and storage) of [consciousness](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Consciousness "Consciousness") which is outside of spacetime. Therefore, perception of nature and the passage of time is illusory at best.|

# [Kardashev Scale](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Kardashev_scale)

- The **Kardashev scale** is a method of measuring a civilization's level of technological advancement, based on the total [energy](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Energy "Energy") usage of a civilization.
- It was proposed in 1964 by [Nikolai Kardashev](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Nikolai_Kardashev "Nikolai Kardashev") and modified in 1973 by [Carl Sagan](https://kardashev.fandom.com/wiki/Carl_Sagan "Carl Sagan") in his book _The Cosmic Connection_, which is the scale we use.
