## Stage III: Actions higher than Tactical

If tactical scenes aren't fast enough (a feat at this current stage), the GM can opt for a **operational scene** instead.

Operational scenes are simply tactical scenes scaled up by a multiplier of 100, 300, 600, or 2400, which represent about 10 minutes, 30 minutes, 60 minutes, or 4 hour increments. At this timescale, the main things to be concerned about are movement and information.
