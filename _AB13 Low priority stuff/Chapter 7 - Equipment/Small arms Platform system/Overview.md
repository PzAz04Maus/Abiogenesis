I'm blocking out a proposal of sorting guns by platform in a way reminiscent of battletechs mech weapons, though less abstract.

The current idea is to sort weapons by platform,  defined by receiver or operating system, then have modifications or variants that modify the base platform, such as short carbine barrel mods. In addition, some variants, particularly older ones,  may grant free attachments such as a dong or light

Any weapon that is virtually identical to the platform or a variant would be simply included as an alias.

# problems

Unless the patterns are done by popularity, there is the major risk to categorize everything by its predecessor, leaving the m60 in the fg42 pattern etc. This would get confusing very fast.

This also would weaken the ability to group things by common controls.

Alternatively, exclusively basing things on action and caliber may be a step too abstract. In such a case it may be a compromise that satisfies neither the purists nor the normies.
