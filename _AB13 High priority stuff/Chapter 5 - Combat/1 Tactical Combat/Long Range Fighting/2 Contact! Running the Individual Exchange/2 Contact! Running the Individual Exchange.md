## Actions on the individual scale versus the tactical scale

Outside of the individual exchange of fire, Teams can engage with intermittent small arms (harassing fire) once per tactical exchange. Only weapons of the same type can be used together.

If more than intermittent gunfire is exchanged in an individual action, the game immediately goes to the individual scale. 

Supporting weapons such as artillery, fire missions, mortars and ATGMs do not act on the individual scale.
