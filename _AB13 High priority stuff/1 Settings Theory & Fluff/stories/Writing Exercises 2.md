# Old Man and Kintpuash

"I want to make a deal." The Old Man broke the silence. "The way I see things, we share the same problem."

"And that is?" the man referred to by his nom de guerre as Kintpuash coolly asked.

"Back before either of us were born, us white people stole your life, your ways from you for gold and land. It's starting to boom like the old ways again, but this time with scum and the desperate showing up to find their own piece of California to profit from, businessmen running us over. It's not gonna be about white and Indian anymore, but us locals and those outsiders filling up the shacks and boomtowns all over us."

"If we're going to control our own lives, we have to join forces on this. Share the money, bury the hatchet." he continued
