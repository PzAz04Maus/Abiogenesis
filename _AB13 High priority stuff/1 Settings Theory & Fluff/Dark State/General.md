You could tell they were finishing off the wounded by the pace of the gunfire. Randy exhaled slowly as he glanced over at the pilot beside him, still breathing but slumped over in a pool of blood with an MPX in his hand. He wasn't going to share the privilege his buddies faced of being executed on American soil by a foreign power - he was going to hold the cards on this one. 

This corner of a ruined house was going to be his mausoleum, he told himself as he pulled the pin on his last remaining grenade, taking care not to let the spoon slip from his bloodsoaked grip as he kept a SIG Spear held on the doorway. These Spetsnaz cocksuckers would have to come through that door and eat shit, just to get a piece of him.

It was an eternity before he started hearing footsteps come through the living room. *This is it*, he choked on his feelings in a desperate rage. *This is where my race on earth has run...* 

A boot crunched in glass, the room clearing crashing to an stop. One of the operators keyed his mic with an agitated voice, trying to smooth things over. The radio's volume spun up to maximum volume, a Russian voice in a icicle stab panic ordering him back.

"Tak Tochna" the Russian replied as the team started to back off Randy's position, but then everything turned to a drywall dust blanket of snow from the walls splintering with autocannon fire, right before a distinctive detonation followed by the ammo cooking off signaled that one of the BTR-82s had catastrophically detonated.

*Reinforcements?!* Randy's heart surged with life. But the source became immediately clear when a thundering roar rose above the sound of a metal chassis being folded in two.
