# Roanoake & Croatoa

On the [Lost Coast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lost_Coast) of Northern California lies Humboldt County. Densely forested, underdeveloped, and mostly Federal land, the region is exceptionally remote for its 138,000 inhabitants.

During the de Kuiper Event, mass communication with California was effectively lost, not only for State or Federal eyes looking in, but for local eyes looking out - the region was cut off from wider society.

For the inhabitants of the Lost Coast, left with all electronics destroyed, it was as if the world had ended, leaving them unable to call out nor escape de Kuiper's many dangers. So they banded together into groups and communities to try to survive, managing to persist to the present day.

For the players of this adventure, the story starts with a tale of two important towns - their home of Croatoa, California and their neighbor, Roanoake, that holds the exit ramp to the Lost Coast. 

Attempting to leave via Roanoake turns into a mystery as they find the town abandoned, the road severed by an eldritch horror. To leave, they must take the lead in solving the mysteries of this new world, or die trying.

The story is based on Zombie Survivor stories such as Day of the Dead or the Walking Dead, though Abiogenesis has no true zombies at this time.
